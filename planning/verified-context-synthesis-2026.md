# Verified Context in 2026: Market & Buyer-Narrative Synthesis for Restormel

## TL;DR
- The 2026 buyer narrative has decisively converged on Restormel's thesis — that production AI stalls on the **trust, quality and provenance of its context**, not model quality — but the dominant framing ("context engineering," Gartner's "Year of Context," context-engine launches) is about *freshness, governance and retrieval*, NOT the *factual verification / span-level provenance* Restormel sells; that gap is simultaneously Restormel's opening and its single biggest risk.
- Restormel's specific moat — span-bound, source-hash-pinned, deterministically re-checkable, cross-model-entailment verification with abstention-to-human — is **not currently shipped as a package by any single named competitor**, and the "verifying MCP proxy" niche is genuinely empty (MCP proxies/gateways do auth, security and governance, never factuality); the nearest encroachers are the grounding/factuality cluster (Contextual AI, Vectara, Patronus/Lynx, Galileo), which do span-level groundedness scoring but not provenance-pinned, re-checkable verification across a knowledge pipeline.
- The regulatory tailwind is real but **nearer-term on data-residency/sovereignty than on EU AI Act applicability**: the Digital Omnibus deferred Annex III high-risk obligations to 2 December 2027, so the only defensible framing is "data-residency + audit-readiness now, high-risk traceability for 2027," not imminent AI Act compliance.

## Key Findings
1. **Demand narrative is mainstream and analyst-backed.** Gartner has formally branded 2026 the "Year of Context" and named context engineering "the breakout AI capability of 2026," with primary-sourced predictions that poor data/context readiness will kill a majority of AI projects. This validates the category but uses "trust" loosely.
2. **Two distinct "trusts" are being sold.** Substrate vendors (Redis Iris, Atlan, memory/graph players) equate "trust" with *freshness/currency/governance*. Independent/academic sources (DeepMind, MIT SMR/BCG) define it as *factual verification + appropriate human reliance*. Restormel sits in the second camp; most enterprise spend is flowing to the first.
3. **The moat is not yet encroached, but the perimeter is closing.** Grounding/factuality vendors do sentence/span-level groundedness scoring; none combine verbatim-span binding + source-version hashing + deterministic re-checkability + cross-model entailment + abstention-to-human as a published, CI-gated quality bar. The verifying-MCP-proxy wedge is empty as of mid-2026.
4. **The human-in-the-loop angle is strongly supported** by exactly the design Restormel uses: DeepMind's amplified-oversight work finds that showing *evidence/search results* fosters more appropriate trust than showing *confidence scores* — a direct endorsement of click-through-to-span over confidence emission.
5. **Regulatory framing should lead with sovereignty/residency.** EU AI Act high-risk duties deferred to Dec 2027; Article 50 transparency largely on track for Aug 2026; UK remains light-touch (no AI Act, DUAA 2025 in force). GDPR/UK-GDPR residency and EU sovereign-cloud momentum are the nearer hooks.
6. **Market sizing is order-of-magnitude only.** AI TRiSM estimates cluster around USD 2.8–4bn in 2025–26 growing ~17–22% CAGR, but definitions vary wildly; treat as directional, not as a TAM.

## Details

### (a) The converging buyer narrative + evidence

**Gartner is the anchor.** Gartner has formally declared 2026 the "Year of Context" and, in its article *Context engineering: Why it's Replacing Prompt Engineering for Enterprise AI Success*, frames context engineering as giving AI "the situational awareness needed to act with relevance and precision," explicitly recommending that context be "continuously updated and validated" and that the context-engineering function integrate with "operational governance (TRiSM) teams." In its 11 March 2026 *Top Predictions for Data and Analytics in 2026* press release, Gartner (quoting Distinguished VP Analyst Rita Sallam) places context, trust and governance at the centre, and its 16 April 2026 release states organisations with successful AI initiatives "invest up to four times more in data and analytics foundations" and that "Agents cannot function autonomously without high-quality context and absolute trust."

The most important Gartner datapoint — now traceable to a **primary** source rather than vendor write-ups — is from the Gartner press release *Lack of AI-Ready Data Puts AI Projects at Risk* (26 February 2025, Roxane Edjlali, Senior Director Analyst): **"Gartner predicts that through 2026, organizations will abandon 60% of AI projects unsupported by AI-ready data,"** paired with a finding that **63% of organisations either do not have, or are unsure if they have, the right data-management practices for AI** (Q3 2024 Gartner survey of 248 data-management leaders). Separately, at the Gartner Data & Analytics Summit 2026 (Orlando, 9–11 March), analyst García-Rodeja warned that **60% of agentic-analytics projects relying solely on MCP will fail by 2028** due to the lack of a consistent semantic layer, and analysts Adam Ronthal and Georgia O'Callaghan declared context "the new critical infrastructure" — with AI-governance-platform spending projected at **USD 492M in 2026, reaching USD 1bn by 2030** (these summit figures reach me via Atlan's conference summary; attributed to named Gartner analysts but verify against primary notes before external quotation).

**Independent/vendor-neutral signals.** Datadog's *State of AI Engineering* (2026) — telemetry-grounded and non-interested — reports that **"context quality — not volume — is the new limiting factor for LLM agents,"** with most teams nowhere near using full context windows. This is a strong corroboration of the "context is the constraint" thesis from an infrastructure-observability vendor with no horse in the verification race. Diginomica's coverage ties context engineering to the much-cited MIT finding on weak enterprise AI ROI.

**Investment-priority shift (vendor-sourced — treat directionally).** VentureBeat's Q1 2026 "VB Pulse RAG Infrastructure Market Tracker" (cited via Redis-adjacent coverage) reports buyer intent to adopt hybrid retrieval **tripled from ~10.3% to ~33.3% between January and March 2026**, with **retrieval optimisation displacing evaluation as the top enterprise AI-infra investment priority for the first time.** This is double-edged for Restormel: spend is moving toward *retrieval* — but toward retrieval *quality/freshness*, not *verification*.

**The "trust" distinction (critical — keep it sharp).** The University of Melbourne–KPMG *Trust, Attitudes and Use of Artificial Intelligence: A Global Study 2025* (Gillespie, Lockey et al.; 48,000+ people across 47 countries; data collected Nov 2024–Jan 2025; DOI 10.26188/28822919) found that **"66% of people use AI regularly, and 83% believe the use of AI will result in a wide range of benefits… [but] only 46% of people globally are willing to trust AI systems,"** that **66% rely on AI output without evaluating accuracy**, and that **70% believe AI regulation is needed.** Trust has *declined* as adoption has risen. This is *human-confidence/adoption* trust — a demand driver for visible verification, but NOT direct evidence of technical context-verification buying. The INSEAD Knowledge piece *The AI Clarity Gap* (Bikard, Mogra, Fernandez-Mateo et al., Feb 2026) argues AI fails on clarity of data, workflow and purpose — adjacent, framing the problem as organisational clarity rather than span-level factuality. Data Center Knowledge's *Why Validation Will Define the Future of HPC and AI* (17 June 2026) is the closest to Restormel's exact framing, arguing validation/verification of AI outputs is becoming a buying differentiator in high-stakes sectors. **Honest read: the convergent narrative is real and broad, but most of it stops one layer short of Restormel's specific claim — that you must verify each output against a provenance-traced source span.**

### (b) Competitive / category state — is the moat encroached? Is the proxy niche empty?

**Grounding/factuality cluster (the real threat to watch).**
- **Contextual AI** ships a "Grounded Language Model (GLM)" with state-of-the-art FACTS-benchmark performance and **inline attributions citing retrieved sources**, plus a groundedness-scoring reward model as a "final layer of protection against hallucination." This is the closest philosophical competitor — attribution + groundedness — but it is a *model + RAG platform*, not a provenance-pinned, deterministically re-checkable verification layer over a BYO knowledge graph, and it does not appear to do cross-model entailment or publish an abstention bar.
- **Vectara** offers "always-on governance" with runtime hallucination detection/correction, citation integrity, on-prem/air-gapped deployment, and its HHEM hallucination-evaluation model/leaderboard (refreshed late 2025 with a harder dataset of 7,700+ articles up to 32k tokens; best rate ~3.3% on the new benchmark vs ~0.7% on the old). Total funding reported at USD 53.5M. Strong regulated-industry positioning; again, runtime scoring, not span-pinned re-checkable provenance.
- **Patronus AI** — the single closest functional overlap. Its CEO has stated "We can actually highlight the span of the specific piece of text where the hallucination is," and its open-source **Lynx** detector (8B/70B) checks RAG groundedness and is self-hostable. Patronus raised USD 17M Series A (total ~USD 20M). Lynx scores groundedness rather than binding claims to source-version hashes for deterministic re-checking — but it is the nearest thing to Restormel's span-level claim in the market.
- **Galileo** (Luna-2 fine-tuned evaluators, sub-200ms inline blocking) is the other live cluster member. **Cleanlab was acquired by Handshake (AI data-labeling company) on 28 January 2026** in an acqui-hire — nine staff including the three MIT-PhD co-founders moved to Handshake's research org; Cleanlab had raised USD 30M total (Menlo Ventures, Bain Capital Ventures, TQ Ventures, Databricks Ventures). **Strategic read: Cleanlab's absorption upstream into training-data quality (serving frontier labs incl. OpenAI) removes a runtime-reliability competitor from the enterprise field — mildly positive for Restormel.**

**Context-engine / context-layer cluster (substrate, mostly commodity).**
- **Redis Iris** (launched 18 May 2026): a context engine with Context Retriever, Agent Memory, Data Integration, LangCache and Search; MCP-native (Context Retriever auto-generates MCP tools). **Crucially, Redis's notion of "trust" = freshness/currency:** its own materials say agents must "trust that [context] is current" and "trust that the context they have is accurate up to the instant." It returns **unverified** context. Redis claims 43% of enterprise AI agent stacks already use Redis somewhere. This is the textbook "substrate vendor crowding the 'context quality' language but not doing verification" — a positioning threat (language collision) more than a capability threat.
- **Atlan** is aggressively colonising "context layer / context engineering" language (Enterprise Data Graph, Context Agents, Context Engineering Studio, Context Lakehouse), citing Gartner heavily and claiming governed context lifts agent accuracy to "94–99% vs 10–31% without it" (Atlan's own marketing figure — flag as vendor-sourced). Governance/metadata, not factual verification.
- **Memory/graph cluster** (Zep/Graphiti, Mem0, Letta, Cognee, Cloudflare Agent Memory): commodity/substrate. Notable: **Zep rebranded itself a "Context Engineering Platform" in 2026**; Graphiti tracks temporal validity windows and preserves provenance "through episodes" — provenance in the *temporal-fact* sense, not the *verbatim-source-span* sense. Mem0 raised a USD 24M Series A (Oct 2025, Basis Set Ventures). Benchmark noise is high (LongMemEval: Zep 63.8% vs Mem0 49.0% on GPT-4o). None verify factuality against source spans.

**MCP gateways / proxies (the wedge — confirmed empty).** Across the 2026 MCP-gateway landscape (MintMCP, Kong, Gravitee 4.10's native MCP-proxy API type, IBM ContextForge, Lasso, MCP Manager, TrueFoundry, PingGateway, Strata), every product governs **auth, RBAC/ABAC, audit logging, rate-limiting, prompt-injection/PII defence, observability** — i.e. *security and governance of tool traffic*. **None return factuality-verified or provenance-pinned context envelopes.** The MCP 2026 roadmap (published March 2026; MCP donated to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation Dec 2025; ~97M monthly SDK downloads) lists gateway/proxy patterns as an *open enterprise-readiness gap* — but framed as auth-propagation/session-affinity, not verification. **Conclusion: the "verifying MCP proxy" niche is genuinely unoccupied as of mid-2026.** This is Restormel's strongest, most defensible strategic claim — and the wide-appeal wedge ("verify the MCPs/KBs you already use") has no direct incumbent.

**Net competitive verdict.** Restormel's *full* moat (verbatim-span binding + source-version hash + deterministic re-checkability + cross-model entailment + abstention-to-human + published ≥90% supported/≤2% unsupported CI-gated bar) is not assembled by any single named competitor today. The encroachment risk is **convergent assembly**: a Patronus-style span detector + a Vectara-style governance runtime + an MCP gateway could, in principle, be stitched into something adjacent. Defensibility rests on the *evidence-bound, deterministically re-checkable* property and the *cross-model* check — which the field currently does not market.

### (c) Human-in-the-loop / responsible-AI positioning angle

This is Restormel's best-supported narrative flank.

- **DeepMind, *Human-AI Complementarity: A Goal for Amplified Oversight*** (Jain, Bridgers, Janzer, Greig, Teh, Mikulik; Google DeepMind; arXiv 2510.26518, v1 30 Oct 2025): the headline finding, verbatim from the abstract, is **"Displaying AI explanation, confidence, and labels leads to over-reliance, but just showing search results and evidence fosters more appropriate trust."** This is a near-perfect academic endorsement of Restormel's design choice to **click-through-to-span (evidence) rather than emit confidence scores.** It also frames "Amplified/Scalable Oversight" — combining humans and AI to supervise AI — and finds that "combining AI ratings and human ratings based on AI rater confidence is better than relying on either alone," directly supporting the abstain-and-route-to-human pattern.
- **MIT SMR × BCG, *Beyond Verification: What Responsible AI Really Demands of Human Experts*** (Renieris, Kiron, Mills, Kleppe; 12 May 2026): **a clear majority (84%) of a panel of 31 AI-strategy experts agree or strongly agree** that "Responsible AI efforts fail if they don't cultivate human experts who can verify AI solutions." The piece prescribes **"a combined system that extends human judgment at scale rather than either replacing or being bottlenecked by it,"** and (per panellist Dawson) "automated evaluation frameworks and agentic assurance pipelines that extend, not replace, human judgment." Recommendation 5 urges treating verification as "a strategic imperative, not just a responsibility practice," noting **86% of top management teams consider AI a significant part of their strategic priorities** (2025 MIT SMR–BCG global executive survey). **Caveat: this article defines "verification" expansively (lifecycle judgment, design, accountability), broader than Restormel's technical span-verification — useful air-cover, but do not conflate.**

**Strategic implication:** Restormel's abstention→human-review + click-through-to-span design is precisely what the most credible independent sources say fosters *appropriate* (not over-) reliance. This is a clean differentiator versus the confidence-score and "freshness=trust" crowds.

### (d) Regulatory / sovereignty framing (with dates)

**EU AI Act — high-risk deferred; lead with this nuance.** On 7 May 2026 the Council and Parliament reached provisional political agreement on the **Digital Omnibus on AI** (Commission proposal published 19 Nov 2025). Key dates (consistent across Gibson Dunn, Hogan Lovells, White & Case, Latham & Watkins, Covington):
- **Annex III stand-alone high-risk obligations deferred from 2 Aug 2026 → 2 December 2027** (16-month slip).
- **Annex I high-risk (embedded in regulated products) deferred → 2 August 2028.**
- **Article 50 transparency obligations largely UNAFFECTED — still apply from 2 August 2026** (chatbot disclosure, deepfake labelling).
- **Article 50(2) watermarking grace period → 2 December 2026** (shorter than the originally proposed six months).
- Formal adoption/Official Journal publication expected before 2 Aug 2026; **dates are not legally binding until adopted** — flag uncertainty.
- The Act's architecture is unchanged; GPAI obligations (Articles 51–55) have applied since Aug 2025 and are untouched.

**Defensible framing for Restormel:** "data-residency now + audit-readiness for the Dec 2027 high-risk traceability/documentation duties," NOT "imminent AI Act applicability." Overclaiming AI Act urgency is now factually wrong given the deferral; the honest pitch is that Restormel's evidence-bound, re-checkable audit trail is *precisely the kind of traceability* high-risk operators will need by Dec 2027 and should build now — every law firm covering the Omnibus says "use the time, don't wait."

**Content-provenance (C2PA) vs context-provenance — keep distinct.** C2PA/Content Credentials (spec v2.3, Feb 2026; 6,000+ members; founders Adobe, Microsoft, BBC, Intel, Arm, Truepic, Sony under the Linux Foundation) cryptographically bind provenance metadata to *media files* (who made it, what edits, AI involvement). It is "tamper-evident provenance, not a truth machine," and adoption is uneven (signing outpaces verification; intermediaries strip metadata; Nikon had to revoke certificates after a signing vulnerability). **This is content/media provenance — a different problem from Restormel's claim-to-source-span context provenance.** Useful analogy ("nutrition label for AI context") but not a competitor or a standard Restormel must adopt. EU AI Act Art. 50 effectively mandates what C2PA provides for AI-generated content.

**UK — light-touch, residency-led.** As of mid-2026 the UK has **no AI Act and no AI Bill before Parliament**; DSIT's 21 Oct 2025 "Blueprint for AI regulation" centres on the AI Growth Lab (sectoral sandboxes), not horizontal statute. Existing regulators (ICO, Ofcom, FCA) apply existing law. The **Data (Use and Access) Act 2025** is in force in stages (bulk of data-protection provisions from 5 Feb 2026, SI 2026/82; new ADM Articles 22A–22D replacing Art. 22; the s.103 complaints procedure commences 19 June 2026). DSIT launched a **£500M Sovereign AI Unit/Fund (16 April 2026)** and a **"Trusted Third-Party AI Assurance Roadmap"** (AI Assurance Innovation Fund, first grants Spring 2026) — directly relevant to Restormel's assurance positioning. **Net: GDPR/UK-GDPR data-residency is the nearer-term commercial hook than AI-specific law.**

**EU sovereign-cloud momentum (supports the self-host/sovereignty angle).** **AWS European Sovereign Cloud went generally available 15 January 2026** (Brandenburg, Germany; €7.8bn; aws-eusc partition; EU-resident-operated German GmbH; ~90 services at launch; BSI C5, SOC 2 Type 1, ISO certs achieved; ~15% price premium). Microsoft (EU Data Boundary, Bleu/Delos JVs, M365 Local GA Feb 2026) and Google (S3NS, Assured Workloads) followed. Gartner (Nov 2025) predicted European IT spend +11% to USD 1.4tn amid sovereignty demand, with 61% of European CIOs wanting more local-cloud use and 53% saying geopolitics would restrict their use of global providers. **This validates Restormel's UK/EU self-host + BYOK + BYO-graph + EU-analytics sovereignty stance as on-trend with board-level buyer concern** — but note the same buyers can now get "sovereign" from hyperscalers, so Restormel's edge must be the *verification layer*, not residency alone.

### (e) Market sizing (order-of-magnitude only)

AI TRiSM (the closest named category) estimates vary widely by definition — treat all as directional:
- **Grand View Research:** USD 2,340.0M (2024) → USD 7,444.9M (2030), **21.6% CAGR** (2025 base est. ~USD 2.80bn).
- **Precedence Research:** USD 2.95bn (2025) → USD 21.06bn (2035), **21.72% CAGR**.
- Multiple secondary trackers cluster the 2025–26 base around **USD 3–4bn**, with CAGRs **~16–22%**.
- Gartner projects organisations operationalising AI TRiSM achieve a **50% reduction in AI-related failures/incidents/violations by 2027** (the ROI argument).
- AI-governance-platform spending: Gartner-attributed figure of **USD 492M in 2026 → USD 1bn by 2030** (via secondary/summit coverage; verify against primary).

Context/RAG infrastructure has no clean single number; the VentureBeat retrieval-priority shift (above) is the best directional signal. **For internal use, treat market sizing as "category exists and is growing ~20%/yr," not as a precise TAM.** Definitional variance between AI TRiSM, AI governance, AI assurance and context infrastructure is too large for a defensible point estimate.

### (f) Honest risks and counter-evidence

1. **Language collision / category capture.** The single biggest risk: "context quality" and "trust" are being defined by *substrate vendors* (Redis, Atlan) as *freshness + governance*, and by analysts (Gartner) as *situational awareness + governance*. If buyers' mental model of "trusted context" becomes "fresh, governed context," Restormel's *factual-verification* meaning may not register as a separate need. Restormel must actively differentiate "verified" from "fresh/governed."
2. **Spend is flowing to retrieval/freshness, not verification.** The Q1 2026 data shows retrieval optimisation displacing *evaluation* as top priority — buyers are prioritising getting *more/fresher* context over *checking* it. This is mild counter-evidence to the "verification is the buy" thesis.
3. **Convergent-assembly encroachment.** No one ships Restormel's full stack, but Patronus (span-level), Vectara (governance runtime + on-prem) and Contextual AI (grounded model + attributions) each hold a piece. A well-funded incumbent could assemble an adjacent offering; the cross-model-entailment + deterministic-re-checkability properties are the thin defensible edge.
4. **Regulatory urgency is softer than it looks.** The Dec 2027 deferral removes near-term AI Act compliance pressure; pitching imminent AI Act need would be inaccurate. The honest pitch is residency-now + audit-readiness-later.
5. **"Trust" surveys measure adoption sentiment, not verification demand.** The KPMG/Melbourne 46%-trust figure is human-confidence, not a procurement signal for technical verification — useful narrative colour, weak as a demand proof point.
6. **Restormel's own efficacy is unverified externally.** The published ≥90% supported / ≤2% unsupported bar is a self-set, CI-gated target; treat as background, not independently validated. Benchmark noise across the field (Vectara old-vs-new datasets; LongMemEval) shows numbers are highly task- and dataset-dependent — Restormel should expect legal/pharma/finance buyers to demand domain-specific evidence, not a headline number.
7. **Sovereign cloud is now table-stakes from hyperscalers.** AWS ESC / Azure / Google sovereign offerings mean Restormel cannot win on residency alone; verification must carry the value.

## Recommendations

**Stage 1 — Sharpen the wedge now (0–3 months).**
- Lead go-to-market with the **verifying-MCP-proxy** ("door 2"): it is the *only* genuinely empty niche and the wide-appeal entry point. Message: "verify the MCP servers/KBs you already use." **Benchmark that changes the plan:** if any MCP-gateway vendor (Kong, Gravitee, MintMCP, IBM ContextForge) ships factuality/groundedness verification, escalate differentiation immediately to the cross-model-entailment + deterministic-re-checkability properties.
- **Own the "verified ≠ fresh/governed" distinction** explicitly. Publish a crisp taxonomy separating Restormel's factual verification from Redis-style freshness and Atlan-style governance. This directly counters the category-capture risk (Risk 1).

**Stage 2 — Anchor the human-oversight narrative (3–6 months).**
- Build the sales narrative on the **DeepMind amplified-oversight finding** (evidence > confidence scores) and the **MIT SMR/BCG 84%** figure. These are the most credible, least self-interested proofs that Restormel's abstain→human + click-through-to-span design is the *correct* design. Position abstention as a feature, not a limitation.

**Stage 3 — Regulatory framing discipline (ongoing).**
- Pitch **"data-residency now + audit-readiness for Dec 2027,"** never "imminent EU AI Act." Map Restormel's evidence-bound audit trail to the Annex III traceability/documentation duties operators must build before 2 Dec 2027. Engage the UK DSIT Trusted Third-Party AI Assurance ecosystem (AI Assurance Innovation Fund) as a credibility/badging route.
- Use sovereign-cloud momentum (AWS ESC GA, board-level EU sovereignty concern) as *context*, but make verification the differentiator — residency is now commoditised.

**Stage 4 — Defend the moat empirically (6–12 months).**
- Pre-empt convergent-assembly encroachment by publishing domain-specific efficacy evidence (legal/pharma/finance) rather than a single headline number — buyers in these verticals will not accept a generic bar. **Benchmark to watch:** if Patronus, Vectara or Contextual AI announce source-version-hash pinning + deterministic re-checkability, treat it as a direct moat threat and accelerate.

**Threshold that would change the overall strategy:** if VentureBeat-style trackers show *verification/evaluation* re-overtaking retrieval as the top infra priority, lean harder into the standalone verified-retrieval product (door 1); if retrieval/freshness continues to dominate spend, keep the proxy wedge (door 2) primary and ride the substrate vendors' rails.

## Caveats
- Several headline statistics (the Gartner summit 60%-by-2028 and USD 492M/USD 1bn governance-spend figures, Atlan's 94–99% accuracy claim, VentureBeat's retrieval-priority tracker) reach me via vendor, conference or secondary write-ups rather than primary analyst notes — flagged inline; verify against primary research before external use. The Gartner "abandon 60% of AI projects through 2026" figure *is* traceable to a primary Gartner press release (26 Feb 2025).
- Market-sizing figures are definitionally inconsistent across firms and should be treated as order-of-magnitude, not TAM.
- EU AI Act Omnibus dates are provisional pending formal adoption and Official Journal publication.
- Restormel's own efficacy bar (≥90% supported / ≤2% unsupported) is treated as a published target, not independently verified.
- Vendor/marketing sources (Redis, Atlan, Vectara, Contextual AI, Patronus, AWS, MCP-gateway vendors) are self-interested and flagged as such; independent/academic sources (DeepMind, MIT SMR/BCG, University of Melbourne–KPMG, Datadog telemetry, law-firm Omnibus analyses) carry more evidential weight.
