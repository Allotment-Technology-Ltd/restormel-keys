> **Filed via Cowork horizon handover, 2026-07-01.** **Placement decision:** the SCHEMA defines no explicit home for standing monitoring criteria, so per the brief these durable tripwires are placed alongside the signal register at `planning/horizon/` as a Tier-1 planning record — reconcile at merge. Each trigger is a condition that, if it fires, changes competitive/strategic posture and should reopen the relevant decision.

## Triggers (durable tripwires)

1. **Mistral's Search Toolkit adds factual verification / provenance re-checking** → co-opetition tips to competition (re HS-014 / Mistral OCR 4).
2. **Contextual AI ships a verifying proxy/gateway**, or a deterministic, re-checkable span-level attribution product.
3. **Any gateway** (Portkey/Prisma AIRS, Kong, IBM ContextForge, AWS AgentCore…) **adds a native groundedness/factuality guardrail** — beyond bolt-on output scoring.
4. **A context-engine incumbent** (Redis Iris, Atlan…) **bolts a groundedness / provenance / factuality layer** onto its context layer.
5. **MCP adds a native multi-tenant model.**
6. **A well-funded incumbent bolts evidence-bound, re-checkable verification onto a context layer.**
7. **Consolidation in the grounding cluster** (cf. Cleanlab → Handshake, Jan 2026).
8. **Material movement in EU AI Act / provenance regulatory dates** — track Official Journal publication of the Digital Omnibus; 2 Aug 2026 stays live until then.
9. **Verification/evaluation re-overtakes retrieval** as the top infra investment priority.
