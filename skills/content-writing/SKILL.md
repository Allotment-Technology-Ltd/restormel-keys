# content-writing

Improve marketing and product copy so it matches Restormel’s brand voice and follows marketing best practice.

## Purpose

Apply the repo’s canonical voice, terminology, and section patterns to landing pages, CTAs, and product copy. Use benefit-led, scannable copy that stays technically credible and on-brand.

## When to use

- Writing or revising marketing landing copy (hero, sections, CTAs).
- Reviewing or improving product/onboarding copy for clarity and impact.
- When asked to “improve the copy” or “align with brand voice.”

## Canonical sources (read first)

- **Positioning:** `docs/01-product-strategy.md` — library-first control layer; provider credentials stay in gateway or env; no default hosted vault.
- **Voice and tone:** `docs/walkthrough/01-writing-style-guide.md` — second person (“you”), present tense, active voice; confident but not cocky; short sentences (15–20 words); no filler (“simply,” “just,” “easily”).
- **Copy registry and CTAs:** `docs/ux-contracts.md` — product nouns (Gateway Key, Dashboard, Sign in, etc.), CTA grammar, key-handling copy.
- **Tone rules:** `docs/reference/restormel-onboarding-copy.md` (§24) — define nouns before configuring; plain English first; tell the user what happens next; powerful but legible.
- **Design principles:** `docs/design-system-index.md` — technical credibility over ornament; calm, analytical; restraint.

## Marketing best practice (apply alongside brand)

- **Benefit-led headlines:** Lead with what the user gains (control, clarity, fewer migrations), not only the product category.
- **One clear value prop:** Hero or first section should state who it’s for and the main benefit in one to two sentences.
- **Single primary CTA:** One main action per section or screen; secondary actions are clearly secondary.
- **Scannable sections:** Short paragraphs; subheads that stand alone; bullet or card lists where it helps.
- **No artifact or placeholder text:** No stray tool names, “Lorem,” or incomplete sentences in live copy.

## Workflow

1. Read the canonical sources above for the surface you’re editing (landing vs dashboard vs docs).
2. Identify the audience and the one thing they should take away from the section.
3. Rewrite in second person, present tense, active voice; cut filler; keep sentences short.
4. Use only terms from the copy registry (Gateway Key, Dashboard, Sign in, etc.).
5. Ensure one primary CTA per section and that the next step is obvious.
6. Check that no sentence is incomplete and no non-product text appears in the UI.

## Outputs

- Revised copy that matches voice and terminology.
- Short note on what was changed (e.g. “Hero: benefit-led headline; Why Keys: one clear value prop; code section: scannable intro”).

## Done criteria

- Copy aligns with docs/walkthrough/01-writing-style-guide.md and docs/ux-contracts.md.
- Headlines and intros are benefit-led and scannable; primary CTA is clear.
- No placeholder or artifact text; no invented product terms.
