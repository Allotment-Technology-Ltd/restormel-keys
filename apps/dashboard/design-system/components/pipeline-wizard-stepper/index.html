PipelineWizardStepper

The numbered progress strip across the top of the Connect ingest setup flow. Each step is completed (solid ink fill, ✓ glyph), active (raised white surface), or upcoming (dashed outline, faint ink). Connectors between two completed steps go solid; every other connector stays dashed. Real per-step completion (not position) drives the ✓ marks, so a deep link to a later step never shows unconfigured steps as done.
