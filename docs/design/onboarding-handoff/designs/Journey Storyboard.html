M1 · variants "Add a model" — the new step, 3 non-opinionated takes

The one screen that didn't exist yet. The job: tell the user what they need (a model to extract, a model to embed) and make registering a key easy — without pushing a provider. Bring Together, Portkey, OpenAI, several keys — their call. Three treatments to compare.
