# REC-DEC-001 — GTM engagement model: invite-only / learning-mode

> Business decision (not an architecture ADR). Captured from the 2026-06-24 horizon-scanning /
> GTM session and filed per the SCHEMA decision-record convention.

## Context

The founder-engagement programme (top-of-funnel for MCP/agent-app founders, per `gtm/ROADMAP.md`
W1-4 and the content engine) was running under the working name **"Founders Circle"**, invite-only.
The session reviewed whether to open self-serve and whether to rename.

## Decision

1. **Keep the engagement model invite-only / learning-mode. Do NOT open self-serve yet.** Early
   access stays curated; the goal this phase is learning and credibility, not self-serve volume.
2. **Rename the programme** away from "Founders Circle." The name is **`[PENDING — founder pick]`**;
   candidates on the table are **"Proving Ground"** and **"Design Partners."** The rename is decided
   in principle; the specific name is the founder's to choose (not invented here).

## Status of each part

- Invite-only / learning-mode, no self-serve — **settled / approved (2026-06-24).**
- Programme rename — **decided in principle; name pending founder selection.** Until the name is
  picked, public-facing copy keeps the current placeholder; nothing renames silently.

## Consequences

- PLG self-serve remains deferred (consistent with the Year-1 investment/learning framing in the
  planning context pack and `gtm/ROADMAP.md`).
- On the name landing, update the references that carry the programme name: `gtm/ROADMAP.md` (W1-4
  invite flow), `gtm/content-calendar.md` (CTAs), `gtm/messaging-foundation.md`, and the two blog
  drafts' CTAs. Route those edits normally; don't hand-edit silently.
- No change to the verified-context headline or the proven-claims-only guardrail.
