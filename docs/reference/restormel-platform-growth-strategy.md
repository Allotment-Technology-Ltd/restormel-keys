# Restormel --- Multi-Product AI Infrastructure Strategy

This document explores the long-term potential for Restormel to evolve
into a multi-product AI infrastructure company.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Long-Term Vision

Restormel could evolve into a modular AI infrastructure platform
composed of multiple developer tools.

Potential ecosystem:

Restormel Keys --- BYOK and provider routing\
Restormel Graph --- reasoning graph debugging\
Restormel Eval --- reasoning evaluation engine\
Restormel Gateway --- optional managed routing infrastructure\
Restormel Governance --- enterprise policy and compliance tooling

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Reality Check

Most developer tools fail.

Typical outcomes:

-   low adoption
-   minimal revenue
-   long growth timelines

Success requires:

strong developer trust\
excellent documentation\
consistent community presence\
multiple useful tools.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Strategic Insight

Restormel should not attempt rapid scaling.

The correct strategy:

build useful tools\
earn developer trust\
expand slowly into infrastructure.

If successful the long-term trajectory could resemble:

Sentry\
Hashicorp\
Supabase

However growth is likely to be slow and incremental.
