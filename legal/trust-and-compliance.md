**Allotment Technology Ltd** (company no. **16925574**, registered in England & Wales;
registered office 71–75 Shelton Street, London, WC2H 9JQ) builds Restormel — the verified-context
layer for AI products. We sell provable trust, so we hold our own posture to the same standard:
provable, documented, and version-controlled.

## Data protection
- Registered with the UK **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** — registration
  **ZC092549**.
- We process personal data under **UK GDPR**. Our Record of Processing Activities, data
  inventory, and retention rules are maintained internally; a **Data Processing Agreement** is
  available for business customers (`/legal/dpa`).
- Sub-processors are listed and kept current at **`/legal/sub-processors`**; material changes are
  notified per our sub-processor change policy.

## Information security management
We operate an information security management system (ISMS), version-controlled and reviewed.
It currently includes an **Information Security Policy**, an **Access Control Policy**, a **risk
register**, a **Record of Processing Activities (RoPA)**, a **supplier/sub-processor register**,
an **asset inventory**, and a **Statement of Applicability** mapped to ISO 27001 Annex A.

## Sovereignty & hosting
Restormel runs **self-hosted in the EU** (Helsinki). We support **UK/EU data residency**,
**bring-your-own-key (BYOK)**, bring-your-own graph store, and **EU-region product analytics** —
so regulated and high-stakes users can keep control of their data.
