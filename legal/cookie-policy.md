## What this covers

Restormel uses a small number of cookies and local-storage entries. Analytics that are **not**
strictly necessary (PostHog product analytics, EU region) run **only with your consent** under PECR
and UK GDPR. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies in the consent banner and change your
choice at any time.

## Categories

- **Strictly necessary** — sign-in/session and security. No consent required.
- **Analytics (PostHog, EU region)** — consent required; off by default until you accept.
